## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 🧠 Machine Learning & AI

> **[neuropeek](https://github.com/NeilP211/neuropeek)** &nbsp;·&nbsp; A mechanistic-interpretability lab that reproduces **and** extends Olsson '22 induction heads on Pythia, with a custom **Triton kernel** for the hot probing path. *Headline result: scale-invariant emergence of induction at training step ~1000.*
> <br/>`Python` `PyTorch` `Triton` `Transformers`

> **[runway](https://github.com/NeilP211/runway)** &nbsp;·&nbsp; An AI stylist that learns what actually *goes together*. A **graph neural network** trained on 35k real Polyvore outfits scores garment compatibility (**0.848 AUC**), powering catalog search, image-extracted color/brand filters, and an interactive "Catwalk" outfit builder.
> <br/>`Python` `PyTorch` `GNN` `Full-stack`

> **[crypt](https://github.com/NeilP211/crypt)** &nbsp;·&nbsp; Geospatial visual search for haunted & abandoned places — drop a photo and a **from-scratch Rust HNSW index** ranks eerily similar locations by CLIP-embedding similarity, distance, and lore.
> <br/>`Rust` `Next.js` `PostGIS` `CLIP` `AWS`

> **[LookBetter](https://github.com/NeilP211/LookBetter)** &nbsp;·&nbsp; A full-stack face-analysis app — FastAPI + Google MediaPipe detect **478 facial landmarks** and score classic proportions (FWHR, symmetry), served to a React frontend.
> <br/>`FastAPI` `MediaPipe` `React` `Python`

### ⚙️ Distributed Systems & Netcode

> **[distkv](https://github.com/NeilP211/distkv)** &nbsp;·&nbsp; A from-scratch **Raft** consensus engine in Go — no etcd/raft library. Leader election, log replication, snapshots, and joint-consensus membership, verified by a deterministic chaos suite and a **linearizability checker**. Ships a live TUI dashboard, Prometheus/Grafana metrics, and Helm/K8s deploy.
> <br/>`Go` `gRPC` `Raft` `Kubernetes`

> **[exfil](https://github.com/NeilP211/exfil)** &nbsp;·&nbsp; A browser-playable authoritative-server extraction shooter exploring competitive-shooter netcode: **64Hz tick**, client prediction, lag compensation, and a measured peeker's-advantage window.
> <br/>`Go` `Rust/WASM` `TypeScript` `three.js`

### 🎮 Games

> **[fortnut-2](https://github.com/NeilP211/fortnut-2)** &nbsp;·&nbsp; A solo-built **Unity (C#)** FPS with custom Blender models, shipped to [GameJolt](https://gamejolt.com/games/fortnut2/551302). The Fortnite parody that taught me to actually finish and ship a game.
> <br/>`Unity` `C#` `Blender`
